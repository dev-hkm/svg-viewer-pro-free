# Privacy Policy for SVG Viewer Pro Free

Last updated: November 02, 2026

**SVG Viewer Pro Free** is built by Khanh Minh as a completely free, open-source Android application. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
SVG Viewer Pro Free operates entirely **offline** on your device.
- We do **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.
- We do **not** use any third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.
- All SVG rendering, editing, recoloring, and image exports occur strictly locally within your device's memory and storage.

## 2. File and Storage Access
The application requires storage permissions only when you explicitly choose to open an SVG file from your storage or save an exported PNG/SVG file to your device's gallery or downloads folder. We never access any files outside of the ones you directly select.

## 3. Security
We value your trust in providing us your files for local viewing. Since no data leaves your device, your private graphics and vector assets remain entirely secure on your hardware.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us via GitHub at [https://github.com/dev-hkm/svg-viewer-pro-free](https://github.com/dev-hkm/svg-viewer-pro-free).
